WispOS replaces your watch's home screen with something leaner and fully open-source.

• Live watch face with complications that actually open things
• Quick settings, real app drawer with recent apps
• Custom face wallpapers (bundled OLED presets or your photos)
• Zero Google Play Services dependency — F-Droid and Aurora Store friendly
• No accounts, no tracking, no background drain

Sideload the APK, set WispOS as your Home app, press the crown. That's the whole setup.
