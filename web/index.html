Why WispOS

We removed
almost everything.

Most smartwatches do too much. They listen when you didn’t ask. They track when you didn’t ask. They get warm in your pocket and tired by dinner.

So we started over and kept only what matters on a wrist this small: the time, at a glance. Your controls, one swipe away. Your apps, where you expect them. Everything else — gone. What’s left is fast, calm, and yours.

◷

Feels instant

Three screens. Left, centre, right. No menus inside menus. If you can swipe, you already know how to use it.

○

Stays out of the way

No assistant waiting for a wake word. No background syncing you never asked for. Just quiet software.

▢

Yours, truly

Install what you like from the open Android world. No forced store, no lock-in. Free and open source, MIT licensed.

Battery & calm

Designed to sip,
not to sip your attention.

WispOS does nothing behind your back. Icons are loaded once and remembered. There are no wake-words, no background trackers phoning home. The result is simple: a watch that stays cool, stays quick, and makes it through your day — and then some.

No listeningNo assistant hotword running day and night
No drainingNo fitness sync unless you open it
No waitingOpens fast, scrolls smoothly, rests deeply

Craft

Every pixel earns
its place.

WispOS is drawn for small round displays — generous type, high contrast, forgiving touch targets. It respects the crown and the home button. It looks right at arm’s length and even better up close.

Built openly. MIT licensed. Shaped by a short, honest roadmap: set as the default home screen, polish motion to a steady glide, then measure real idle drain — and publish the numbers.
