# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, condensed)

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in WispOS a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards
- Be welcoming, respectful, and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Give feedback on code, not people; assume good intent
- No harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized language, or personal attacks
- Respect differing viewpoints; disagree without demeaning

## Enforcement
Violations can be reported to the maintainers via a GitHub issue or discussion.
Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors who do
not follow this Code of Conduct. All reports are handled confidentially.
